Questions

Baling machine FAQs

Quick answers to what customers ask us most.

Who manufactures baling machines in Johannesburg?

SVN Engineering & General Works manufactures all types of baling machines in Johannesburg – vertical, horizontal, cardboard, plastic, PET, scrap metal, can and textile balers – and delivers them across South Africa. Call or WhatsApp 076 051 8003.

How much does a baling machine cost in South Africa?

It depends on the baler type, bale size, press force and whether it's manual or automatic. A small vertical cardboard baler costs far less than a fully automatic horizontal baler. We give free, no-obligation quotes based on your material and volumes.

Which baler is best for cardboard and plastic?

For shops, warehouses and small recyclers, a vertical hydraulic baler is usually the best fit for cardboard and plastic film. High-volume plants and distribution centres normally need a horizontal baler with conveyor feeding.

Do you repair and service baling machines?

Yes. We repair and service most makes of balers – hydraulic cylinders, pumps, seals, electrical controls, door locks and chamber wear plates – throughout Johannesburg and Gauteng.

Can you build a custom baling machine?

Yes. Every machine is fabricated in-house, so bale size, chamber opening, tonnage, power supply (single or three phase) and safety features can be built around your site and material.

Do you deliver balers outside Gauteng?

We do. We're based in Johannesburg and deliver and install balers nationwide, including Pretoria, Durban, Cape Town, Polokwane, Bloemfontein and Mbombela.
